Support

Shiftalya helps shift workers plan rotations, track hours, and import schedule PDFs on the device. The app is designed to work without an account and without sending your shift data to our servers.

For questions, feedback, or privacy requests, contact us by email. We aim to respond within a reasonable time.

Privacy policy contact.bar2run@gmail.com

Quick facts

  • Data stays on your phone unless you choose to share it elsewhere.
  • PDF import reads files on-device; we do not upload your PDFs.
  • Reminders are local notifications — optional and controlled in Settings.
  • No ads, in-app purchases, or analytics SDKs in the current app version.
